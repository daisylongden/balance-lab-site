The problem

In the impact space, there is always something more important than fixing your processes. So the inefficiencies stay, and capacity remains capped.

  • Teams are stretched

    Hours go to admin, reporting and repeat questions instead of the people you exist to serve.

  • AI feels urgent but unclear

    Staff are already trying tools on their own, without shared rules on data, ethics or what good looks like.

  • Nobody has time to zoom out

    Fixing how work flows needs someone outside the day-to-day who can map it, test changes and see them through.

How we work

A process, not a project

We work alongside your team at every step. Each one leaves you better able to find and fix the next bottleneck yourselves.

  1. 01

    Zoom out

    We map how work really flows, where time goes, and what your team wishes they could spend it on.

  2. 02

    Co-create

    We design the fix with the people who will use it, so they fit your workflows and your values.

  3. 03

    Build skills in-house

    We install the tools and train a named internal AI lead, so the know-how stays when we leave.

  4. 04

    Report on what works

    We measure against a baseline and show plainly what is saving time and what isn't.

  5. 05

    Fix what doesn't

    We adjust or drop what isn't earning its place, then look at the next challenge together.

Questions we hear

Before you get in touch

Will AI replace people on our team?

That isn't the goal. We look for work that drains time without adding value, so your people can spend more of it on the work only they can do.

What happens to our data?

We check what data is involved before anything is built, and agree red lines with you in week 1. Sensitive information stays out of tools that aren't fit for it.

We're small. Is this for us?

Our sprints suit organisations with roughly 10 to 150 staff that have at least one process that eats far too much time.

Can we pay for this from a grant?

Many organisations do, from their own budget or an existing grant line. If you're applying for new funding, we can help as an add-on.

What type of organisations do you work with?

Mission-driven organisations in the Netherlands: charities, foundations, social enterprises and member networks, usually with 10 to 150 staff. We focus on six areas, from poverty and debt to education, health and inclusion. See who we work with.

Do you have guidelines for how you use AI?

Yes. We keep AI's harm as low as we can while giving your team more time. That means six red lines we won't cross, such as scoring or ranking people for support, and nine commitments we keep on every project. Read how we use AI.

What happens if AI tools get more expensive?

They might, and some already have. That's why we check every workflow against three times today's running cost before we build it. We also test a second tool where we can, and write down how your team would work without AI. If prices change, you'll have a clear plan, not a crisis. And if prices fall, we'll show you at your month-3 check-in.
