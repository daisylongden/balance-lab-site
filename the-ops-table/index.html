Why this table exists

Every organisation I've worked in had the same problems

I've spent the last 10 years building better systems for mission-driven organisations: NGOs, social initiatives and companies with a purpose.

In each one, the fixes that would win back the most time kept getting pushed aside by whatever was urgent that week. Everyone agreed things should change. Making room for the change was the hard part.

Our sector talks a lot about working together to make change happen. The Ops Table does the same for the people behind the scenes.

We share what's working, what isn't, and the wins worth copying, so nobody has to start from scratch. And it doesn't stop when breakfast ends.

What it is

A community, not a one-off event

Each month you bring your own pain points and work through them with fellow ops leaders. A simple framework helps you see your challenges in new ways and learn from how other organisations have tackled theirs.

Keep connected through the community chat and join the roundtables that feel relevant to you.

The goal? Take away tangible ideas and workshop existing plans with a community tackling the same challenges.

How the table works

Five things that stay the same

The format may change from table to table. These ground rules don't.

  1. What's said here stays here. Share ideas, not names.
  2. Real problems over polished answers. Progress over perfection.
  3. Ask before you advise. One good question beats five quick fixes.
  4. No selling, from anyone. Not from us, not from attendees. Everyone is here to learn.
  5. Leave with one thing to try. Small and specific, so you can start on Monday.

Questions

Before you sit down

Is it really free?

Yes. Breakfast is on us, and there's nothing to buy at the table.

Will you try to sell me something?

No. There's no pitch in the room or in the WhatsApp group. If you'd like to talk about your own work afterwards, you can ask for a free intro call.

Do I need to know about AI?

No. We start with your problems. AI comes up where it might help, and we're just as honest about where it shouldn't.

Can I bring a colleague?

Yes, up to 2 people per organisation. Each session has 10 seats, so it stays small enough for everyone to speak.

Do I have to come every month?

No. Come when it suits you. Each table works on its own, and you stay part of the group in between.

What happens between tables?

The group stays open. Members ask questions, share drafts and report back on what they tried. With your permission, we share the ideas and wins in our recaps, so good work gets seen by more teams.
