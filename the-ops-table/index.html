Why this table exists

Every organisation I've worked in had the same problems

I've spent my whole career in mission-driven work: NGOs, social initiatives and companies with a purpose.

In each one, the fixes that would win back the most time kept getting pushed aside by whatever was urgent that week. Everyone agreed things should change. Making room for the change was the hard part.

In a small team you're often the only one pushing. So every new process becomes its own project: bringing colleagues along, one conversation at a time.

Because the problems are so alike, the answers travel well.

Our sector asks the people we serve to work together to make change happen. The Ops Table does the same for the people who run our organisations. We share what's working, what isn't, and the wins worth copying, so nobody has to start from scratch.

What it is

A peer table, not an event

Each month you bring your own pain points and work through them with a group of fellow ops leaders. A simple framework helps you see your challenges in new ways and learn from how other organisations have tackled theirs.

The goal? Get inspired and leave with a plan you can take back on Monday.

Who it's for

A seat for the people who keep things moving

  • People leading mission-driven work inside companies: impact, sustainability, foundation or community programmes.

  1. What's said here stays here. Share ideas, not names.

Questions

Before you sit down

Is it really free?

Yes. Breakfast is on us, and there's nothing to buy at the table.

Will you try to sell me something?

No. There's no pitch in the room or in the WhatsApp group. If you'd like to talk about your own work afterwards, you can ask for a free intro call.

Do I need to know about AI?

No. We start with your problems. AI comes up where it might help, and we're just as honest about where it shouldn't.

Can I bring a colleague?

Yes, up to 2 people per organisation, so there's room for about 10 organisations.

Do I have to come every month?

No. Come once and decide later. Each session works on its own.
