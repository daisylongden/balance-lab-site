Balance Lab / Who we work with

Who we work with, and what gets in their way

We work with charities, foundations, social enterprises, member networks, and companies with a mission. The pressures differ, but the same jobs tend to eat a team's time.

Why not any company? Because time is precious and we would rather use ours to help organisations creating a positive impact.

Across the sector

Six pressures almost everyone we talk to shares

Reporting load

Every funder wants its own format, and the same numbers get rewritten again and again.

Volunteers to coordinate

Hundreds or thousands of volunteers who need onboarding, answers and scheduling from a small paid team.

Plain language

Information has to work for everyone, often at B1 level or below, and in more than one language.

Tighter budgets

Rising demand, and in development work, Dutch government funding cuts from 2026.

Sensitive data

Case notes, health and money worries. One careless tool choice can break trust.

Access to information

Information is spread across many sources, so staff and communities lose time finding what they need.

Sound familiar?
