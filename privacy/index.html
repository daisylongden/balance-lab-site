Balance Lab / Privacy

How we handle your data

We collect as little as we can, use it only for what we told you, and remove it when you ask.

Who we are

Balance Lab is a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) in Amsterdam, run by Daisy Longden, registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce (KvK) under number 97751154. The Ops Table is an initiative from Balance Lab. Balance Lab decides how your data is used and is responsible for it.

Questions or requests: hello@balance-lab.nl.

When you join The Ops Table community

  • What we collect: your name, role, organisation and email address, plus the date you signed up and the consent text you agreed to.
  • Why: to email you when registration opens, and about Ops Table events and activities. Nothing else.
  • Our legal basis: your consent. You give it by ticking the box in the sign-up form.
  • How long we keep it: until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove you, or until The Ops Table ends.
  • Where it's kept: in a database hosted by Supabase.

When you send us a message

  • What we collect: your name, organisation, email address and your message.
  • Why: to reply to you and plan an intro talk if you ask for one.
  • Our legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering the people who contact us.
  • How long we keep it: for as long as we're in conversation, and up to 12 months after.
  • Where it goes: the form is delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit. Our email is hosted by Zoho Mail.

When you visit this site

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no cookies and builds no profile of you. That's why you don't see a cookie banner.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub briefly logs technical details such as your IP address to keep the service secure.

What we never do

We don't sell your data, share it for advertising, or add you to mailing lists you didn't ask for. The companies named on this page only handle your data to provide their service to us.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete your data, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Email hello@balance-lab.nl and we'll act within one month. Every Ops Table email also has an unsubscribe option.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
